# Personal Branding & Identity

## Candidate Profile
* **Name:** Jane Smith
* **Professional Summary:** Jane Smith is a Senior Product Manager with 10 years of experience in B2B SaaS.

## Identity Rules
* **Voice:** Third-person objective (e.g., "Jane led...").
* **Tone:** Professional, data-driven, and strategic.

## Strategic Voice
* Emphasize business impact and revenue growth over pure technical execution.
* Always quantify achievements with metrics (e.g., "Increased user retention by 15%").
